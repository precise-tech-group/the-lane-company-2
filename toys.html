Reviewed for safety before listing

Material

We start with what it's made of.

Solid woods, organic textiles, food-safe finishes. If a cheaper material would compromise the feel in the hand, we pass.

Safety

Then, whether it's truly safe for small hands.

Every piece is reviewed for choking hazards, edge finishing and non-toxic pigment before it earns a place in the catalog.

Durability

Built to survive more than one child.

We favor joinery and stitching that holds up to years of play — and, ideally, a second owner down the line.

Delight

Finally — does it earn a second glance?

The objects we choose reward curiosity. If it doesn't make us want to pick it up and play, it doesn't make the cut.
